Installable in one command on three platforms, with persistent stats and a solver that vets deals.

how they are doing. Prebuilt binaries for the three desktop platforms, crates.io
and Homebrew, persistent statistics, and a README with a recording.

Also the first place where the engine earns its separation: a solver that checks
a deal is winnable before offering it.

Early December so it is out before the holidays, when people actually play
solitaire.
